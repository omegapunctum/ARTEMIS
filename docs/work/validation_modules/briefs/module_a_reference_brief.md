## Question

How were ancient and earlier domed precedents selectively reinterpreted in Renaissance and later European architecture, and which connections are documented rather than inferred from visual resemblance?

## Compared Features

- **Pantheon (Rome)** — Provides the ancient centralized domed precedent explicitly named by the Villa Rotonda evidence and used as a limiting comparison for St. Peter's.
- **Brunelleschi’s Dome (Florence Cathedral)** — Separates documented construction innovation from unsupported assumptions that every later masonry dome derives directly from the Pantheon.
- **St. Peter's Basilica** — Supplies the explicitly cited dome model for Soufflot and a contested scholarly comparison with the Pantheon.
- **Villa Almerico Capra “La Rotonda”** — Offers an official, unusually explicit statement of derivation from the Pantheon together with evidence that the executed dome differs from Palladio's published design.
- **Panthéon (Paris)** — Provides a later documented act of modelling and competitive ambition directed toward St. Peter's rather than a generic classical resemblance.

## Lenses

- form and composition

### A-C4 · interpretation · origin curator · review reviewed · medium confidence · supported

### A-C5 · interpretation · origin curator · review reviewed · medium confidence · supported

## Conclusion

Status: `provisional`

The module supports two selective, source-named acts of derivation: La Rotonda from the Pantheon's centralized domed conception and Soufflot's Panthéon from aspects of St. Peter's. It does not support a continuous Pantheon-to-Florence-to-St. Peter's-to-Paris genealogy; construction, execution history and competing models interrupt that shortcut.

## Uncertainty

- The package does not claim that every formal resemblance among the five domes records transmission.
- The attribution boundary between Palladio's design and Scamozzi's executed dome requires specialist review.
- The Campbell interpretation should be checked against additional St. Peter's scholarship before external validation.
