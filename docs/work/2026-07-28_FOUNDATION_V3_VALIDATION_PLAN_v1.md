# ARTEMIS — FOUNDATION v3 VALIDATION PLAN v1

## Статус

- Тип: active working validation contract.
- Дата: 2026-07-28.
- Parent: issue `#327`.
- Stage: design; implementation and participant recruitment are not authorized by this document alone.
- Vertical: `Life in Context`.

## 1. Decision question

> Даёт ли synchronized spatial-temporal world model измеримое улучшение contextual understanding и discovery по сравнению с теми же материалами без синхронизированной карты, времени и слоёв?

## 2. Product hypotheses

Проверяются H1–H7 из `PRODUCT_THESIS.md`:

- contextual configuration;
- simultaneity discovery;
- understanding of trajectory/change;
- relation literacy;
- trust/uncertainty;
- layer usefulness;
- sustainable World Slice curation.

## 3. Test content

One frozen Leonardo World Slice:

- 1452–1519 overall bounds;
- selected stops/time windows only;
- fixed content inventory;
- identical sources and narrative text in both conditions;
- explicit coverage statement;
- reviewed Claim/Evidence fixtures;
- uncertainty fixtures;
- relation-ladder fixtures.

Content freeze occurs before participant sessions.

## 4. Conditions

### A — ARTEMIS synchronized experience

- map and timeline share one state;
- trajectory, Events, Regions/States and layers update with time;
- local/global context views;
- relation-ladder labels;
- source/locator/uncertainty access.

### B — same-content baseline

- same texts, media, sources and locators;
- chronological/list/detail navigation;
- no synchronized map/time/layer interaction;
- no content advantage or extra explanation.

### C — normal workflow benchmark

Optional short benchmark using the participant's normal method. It is not allowed to receive more time or sources than the main tasks.

## 5. Participants

Initial controlled wave:

- 6–8 primary users;
- students or serious independent learners;
- no prior ARTEMIS training;
- mixed familiarity with Leonardo/Renaissance recorded;
- recruitment and exclusions frozen before scoring.

A second wave requires an `ITERATE` decision, not silent protocol expansion.

## 6. Tasks

Participants must:

1. reconstruct where the person was and what characterized the local context at selected times;
2. identify meaningful simultaneous events locally and globally;
3. explain one trajectory/context change;
4. classify examples as co-presence, possible encounter, documented encounter, interaction or influence;
5. locate the basis and uncertainty for selected statements;
6. provide a short synthesis without causal overclaim.

## 7. Measures

### Context reconstruction

- correct place/time;
- correct regional state;
- relevant Events/Processes;
- false additions;
- omission of material context.

### Simultaneity discovery

- number of relevant synchronous elements found;
- whether element was previously unknown to participant;
- explanation of relevance;
- false relation claims.

### Change/trajectory

- ordering and duration;
- route/segment uncertainty;
- state/process change comprehension.

### Relation literacy

Critical error:

- co-presence or similarity stated as documented encounter/influence/causality without evidence.

### Trust

- source/locator retrieval;
- recognition of challenge/uncertainty;
- confidence calibration.

### Behavior

- navigation path;
- map/time/layer interactions;
- ignored or confusing layers;
- time to first contextual insight;
- optional delayed recall after 3–7 days.

### Cost

- curator hours;
- reviewer hours;
- unresolved items;
- source/licensing blockers;
- implementation/adaptation cost.

## 8. Scoring

- fixed rubric;
- blinded scoring of written outputs where possible;
- two evaluators for relation/causal claims;
- disagreement resolved and recorded;
- absolute counts reported alongside percentages;
- qualitative insights cannot erase critical-error counts.

## 9. Minimum success signal

An `EXPAND ONE BRANCH` decision requires all:

- clear improvement in context reconstruction over baseline;
- more relevant simultaneity discoveries without more critical relation errors;
- users successfully interpret trajectory/change;
- source/uncertainty access works for material Claims;
- at least two layers have explainable observed contribution;
- no result depends on promised AI/3D/VR;
- curation cost is measured and judged repeatable.

Threshold values must be frozen after pilot dry-run and before participant wave.

## 10. Possible decisions

- `ITERATE` — core shows promise but content/UI/semantics need another controlled version;
- `EXPAND ONE BRANCH` — name exactly one next branch;
- `NARROW VERTICAL` — world-model mission remains, but Leonardo/scope/UI is changed;
- `STOP/RETHINK` — current approach does not demonstrate useful spatial-temporal understanding.

Failure of one vertical does not authorize replacing the mission with Concept v2. It requires a new vertical/interface/content decision.

## 11. Readiness gates

### Contract ready

- world-model fixtures pass review;
- relation ladder is unambiguous;
- temporal/spatial uncertainty represented;
- current compatibility limitations listed.

### Content ready

- selection rationale and coverage frozen;
- sources and locators reviewed;
- no invented route/geometry;
- alternative/disputed items labeled;
- cost log complete.

### Experience ready

- synchronized state is deterministic;
- required desktop/tablet/mobile flows work;
- same-content parity verified;
- no hidden content advantage;
- source and uncertainty accessible.

### Study ready

- assignment, timebox and scoring frozen;
- recruitment/exclusion rules frozen;
- evaluators trained;
- dry run completed without changing outcome thresholds afterward.

## 12. Evidence package

Store:

- frozen content manifest;
- condition manifests;
- coverage statement;
- source/review registry;
- rubric;
- participant anonymized outputs;
- interaction/session logs where consent allows;
- evaluator scores/disagreements;
- critical errors;
- curation cost;
- final decision and rationale.

## 13. Non-goals

- usability-only validation;
- visual preference test;
- proof of universal ontology;
- proof of AI reasoning;
- proof of 3D/VR value;
- content popularity;
- growth/retention claims;
- public launch.
