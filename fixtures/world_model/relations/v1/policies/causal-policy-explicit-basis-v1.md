# Synthetic explicit-basis causal policy v1

This policy is an executable contract fixture. It has no historical authority and is not approval for
production causal assertions. A production corpus must replace it with a separately governed policy and
independently reviewed evidence.

The fixture accepts a causal RelationClaim only when all of the following are explicit and source-bound:

1. the causal Claim and its distinct causal-basis Claim have the same target, subject and object;
2. both Claims have reviewed, non-background supporting EvidenceLinks;
3. the causal Claim states direction, mechanism, bounded scope and a counterfactual basis;
4. the basis Claim names the comparison or counterfactual contrast used by the assertion;
5. the conclusion is limited to the declared scope and does not imply universal or sole causation.

The fixture forbids promotion from co-presence, encounter, interaction or influence alone. It also forbids
using sequence, correlation, shared classification, similarity or an unresolvable policy identifier as a
causal basis.

The only accepted fixture policy identifier is `causal-policy-explicit-basis-v1`. Its record and this file
are both inside the Relation v1 normalized review scope.
