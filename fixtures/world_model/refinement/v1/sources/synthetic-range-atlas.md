# Synthetic range atlas

This fixture source exists only to make Claim-level locators reproducible. It has no historical
authority.

## RANGE-1900-COARSE

raw: `broad 1900 range envelope`

claim: The synthetic species occupied the coarse 1900 range envelope.

## RANGE-1900-REFINED

raw: `supported 1900 subregional range`

claim: The synthetic species occupied the narrower supported 1900 range envelope.

## RANGE-2000

raw: `2000-2010 range state`

claim: The synthetic species occupied a different range during 2000 through 2010.

## REGION-A

raw: `reconstruction A`

claim: Synthetic reconstruction A covers one possible 1502 region envelope.

## REGION-B

raw: `reconstruction B`

claim: Synthetic reconstruction B is a competing 1502 region envelope.
